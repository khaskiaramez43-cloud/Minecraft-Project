Build, collect and place tiles in a simple 2D Minecraft world.

How to Play

1. Select a Tool

Choose the correct tool from the toolbar.

2. Remove a Tile

Click a tile that matches the selected tool.

3. Collect the Tile

The removed tile will be added to your inventory.

4. Place It Back

Select a tile from the inventory and place it in an empty space.

Tools Guide

  • Axe: removes leaves and tree trunk tiles.
  • Pickaxe: removes rock tiles.
  • Shovel: removes dirt and grass tiles.
